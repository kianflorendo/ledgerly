# Evidence and approval policy

Canonical policy. Backend guards and contract review were exercised in the 2026-11-05 regression run; see the verification record for the limits of that evidence.

- A merged PR demonstrates a development event, not contractual acceptance.
- Match evidence to the exact customer, project, milestone and relevant contract version.
- Acceptance must satisfy the contract and come from a known authorized actor. Display names alone are insufficient.
- Store authority mapping with provenance; do not let the model invent approvers.
- Supported means evidence satisfies the documented requirement; missing, conflicting and unknown are distinct outcomes.
- A contract upload starts unknown, not supported. The current workflow supports billing conditional on delivery and written client acceptance. A local reviewer must confirm an exact clause documenting that condition. Other conditions remain under internal review.
- Contract review records source content/version and case-context hashes, exact quote, declared reviewer and timestamp. Source, milestone, invoice, project or authority changes require review again. Keyword eligibility checks alone never grant supported status; the reviewer must attest to the clause. This is a recorded human interpretation, not legal advice or authenticated reviewer identity.
- Every factual finding needs an existing source ID, exact excerpt, page/URL, source timestamp and version/hash.
- Validate source existence and matching excerpts in code. Citation validity alone does not establish interpretation correctness.
- Supported model citations must use the current policy-selected source of the matching requirement type and remain inside its reviewed passage. Contract quotes must together cover billing, delivery and written acceptance; a heading or invoice identifier alone is insufficient. These coverage checks do not establish unrestricted semantic entailment.
- Unreviewed or stale uploaded acceptance evidence blocks reliance on older acceptance until reviewed. A reviewed rejection remains conflicting; an explicit not-acceptance review dismisses that record, clears prior investigation/approval and requires reinvestigation.
- Treat documents, issue text and emails as untrusted data; embedded instructions cannot override tools or policy.
- A reconciliation draft uses neutral, cooperative language. No claims of client dishonesty or legal conclusions.
- Human approval covers exact recipients, message body, attachments and evidence versions.
- Any material change invalidates approval. Recheck current state immediately before execution.
- Draft editing, approval and sending require a completed, successful, current investigation containing exactly one valid contract, delivery and acceptance finding. Empty, incomplete, duplicate, failed or stale findings are rejected. Both approval and sending independently restrict unresolved evidence to internal review.
- Editing, approval, sending and PDF export share current investigation validation. Previously saved model output and displayed citations are checked against the current citation requirements; legacy heading-only findings require reinvestigation instead of being grandfathered into a current pack.
- Retry/double-click must not duplicate delivery. Fail closed on uncertain authorization.
- Keep prepared, approved, sent, blocker-cleared and paid outcomes separate.
- Synthetic data, local inference, live lookup, cached snapshot and test email must be visibly labeled.
- A COA/COC requirement is contract/customer-specific.
- BIR Form 2307 is withholding-tax documentation, not universal acceptance proof or a universal payment prerequisite. See [research](research.md).
- Do not claim immutable/tamper-proof auditing from an ordinary local event log.
